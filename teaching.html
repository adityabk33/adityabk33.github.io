Teaching

I want students to leave able to set up a problem, question the assumptions behind a model, read their results honestly, and explain their reasoning to someone else.

In Spring 2023, I taught introductory mechanics to more than 200 students as the course's sole instructor. I designed the lectures, homework, exams and grading, and led the team of teaching assistants.

How I teach

Intuition before formulas

Students explain why a calculation fits before they do it. In a statistics course, I'd start regression from the question and the plot, then build to assumptions, uncertainty and diagnostics.

Learning from each other

Students explain their solutions to the group, and I guide the discussion from there. Questions and half-formed ideas are treated as a normal part of learning, for students of every background.

Beyond the classroom

In the lab, data is noisy and equipment misbehaves, so I treat discrepancies as information to interpret. My research on privacy and machine learning gives real examples of the same judgment.
